AI-Native Investment Banking Platform

The Firm's Automated Institutional Memory & Intelligence Engine

What Are We Building?

We are not building a simple chatbot that answers questions on demand. We are building a continuously updated enterprise intelligence platform.

The Golden Rule: The AI (LLM) is never the source of truth. The database is. The AI simply acts as a smart parser that reads messy documents and turns them into organized data. It never guesses.

The Core Problem

Traditionally, analysts spend countless hours hunting through news, filings, and websites to find data, updating spreadsheets manually. That knowledge is siloed, easily lost, and hard to verify.

The Solution

Tireless AI "workers" scour the internet 24/7. They find raw documents, extract verifiable facts (revenue, leadership changes, new products), and save them to a central database with exact source receipts.

1. Collection (The Workers)

Independent "Worker" bots run constantly. They only collect; they do not analyze. They monitor:

  • Company Websites: Detecting changes in products or pricing.
  • News & Press: Scanning Reuters, Bloomberg, and financial media.
  • Registries: Pulling corporate filings, incorporation dates, and capital.
  • Platforms: Checking GitHub activity and LinkedIn hiring trends.

Progress & Roadmap

The core infrastructure is fully functional. The system is actively crawling, reading, extracting, and verifying data autonomously. Click below to expand the details.

  • Foundation: Database, storage, and NVIDIA AI models are live. Master company schema (103 data points) is established.
  • The Workers: Automated schedulers are running 8 different data workers (Web, News, GitHub, LinkedIn, Registries, Patents, Gap-Fillers).
  • Analyst Tools: Telegram bot is live for Q&A and transcript uploads. Web Dashboard is built to review facts and company profiles.
  • Smart Search: Semantic search engine is live, allowing analysts to search through all collected documents instantly.
  • Public vs. Private Financials: Adapted the system to recognize that Indian company financials (Revenue, EBITDA) are publicly available via registries and news, unlocking massive data automation (up to ~60% fill rate).
  • Precision Extraction: Fixed AI tendencies to write conversational "stories". It now strictly outputs pure, formatted data points for the database.
  • Conflict Resolution: Built an automated "Judge" that ranks sources (e.g., an official transcript always overrides a blog post).

Now that we have a trusted, continuously updating database, we will build Analysis Agents. These agents will sit on top of the data and automatically generate:

  • Valuation models (DCF / Comps)
  • Competitive Intelligence reports
  • Automated Investment Thesis drafts (bull/bear with linked evidence)
  • Risk Summaries (litigation, concentration, leadership changes)
